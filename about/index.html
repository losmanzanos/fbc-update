Who We Are

About Full Bloom Counseling

A Denver group practice built around therapists who love their work, and clients who can feel the difference from session one.

Our Story

Built on the Belief That You Deserve to Be Seen

Full Bloom Counseling was founded by Becca Moravec with a conviction that too many people were receiving good-enough therapy in places that didn't feel good enough. She wanted to build something different: a practice where clinicians genuinely loved their work, where care was never rushed, and where clients felt that from the very first session.

What started as a solo practice has grown into a team of seven carefully chosen therapists who share that standard. We're a group private practice, which means you get the intimacy and continuity of your own dedicated therapist, backed by the collective knowledge of a whole team that consults, challenges each other, and holds each other accountable.

The name is intentional. Full bloom isn't where you start. It's where you're headed.

In the Community

Care That Travels With Your Child

Full Bloom Counseling Group is the mental health partner for Colorado Children's Chorale, one of the nation's premier children's choral organizations, based right here in Denver. We embed a dedicated clinician on every Chorale tour, from Colorado performances to international concert tours.

Becca Moravec, LPC, LMFT developed a custom mental health framework built specifically for the demands of touring with young singers. It gives Chorale leadership and clinicians a shared language and a real plan, not a generic protocol.

What Guides Us

Our Clinical Values

These aren't marketing points. They shape every clinical decision we make, every therapist we hire, and every session we hold.

Trauma-Informed First

Anxiety, relationship patterns, body image struggles and addictive behaviors often trace back to experiences that shaped the nervous system. That’s where we look first.

Body-Affirming & HAES

We don’t set weight goals or give diet advice. The practice follows Health at Every Size principles and an anti-diet approach to food and bodies.

LGBTQ+ Affirming

We are affirming of all identities, orientations and relationships. You won’t have to explain or defend who you are to your therapist.

Evidence-Based

Our training includes EMDR, the Safe and Sound Protocol, the Gottman Method, EFT, the Narrative Enneagram, Internal Family Systems and somatic work. We use what the research supports and what helps the person in front of us.

Collaborative Practice

Your therapist isn’t working alone. We meet regularly to consult, bring hard cases to the whole group, and hold each other to a high standard.

Flexible Availability

Evening and weekend hours, a free 15-minute consultation before you commit, and sessions in person or by secure video anywhere in Colorado.
