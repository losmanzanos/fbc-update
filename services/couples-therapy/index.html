Couples therapy for when you keep having the same fight

Every couple fights. What matters is whether you can get through it without leaving damage behind. We help you see your pattern clearly enough to change it, together.

The fight under the fight

Different topics, same dance

Most stuck couples aren’t really fighting about the dishes or the in-laws. One partner gets scared of losing connection and reaches harder. The other feels like they’re failing and backs away to keep the peace. Each move makes the other one worse.

Couples therapy slows that loop down until you can both see it. Once you can name the cycle together, it stops running you.

Who comes to see us

You don’t need to be in crisis

Some couples come in close to the edge. Others are doing fine and want to stay that way.

  1. Communication breakdown

    The same argument over and over: different topics, same pattern underneath, and nobody wins.

  2. Trust repair

    Rebuilding after infidelity, a significant breach, or years of small disconnections.

  3. Life transitions

    A new baby, a career change, a move: the kind of stress that exposes cracks that were already there.

  4. Growing apart

    Feeling more like roommates than partners, and wanting to find your way back.

How we work

The methods we lean on

Most of our couples work draws on one or more of these, depending on the couple and the therapist.

The Gottman Method

One of the most rigorously researched approaches to couples work. It helps you understand the patterns under your conflict and build something more durable.

Emotionally Focused Therapy

Grounded in attachment science. We get underneath the cycle to the fear and longing driving it.

The Enneagram

Getting started

The first few weeks

  1. A free 15-minute call

    Either of you can make it. We’ll talk through what’s going on and match you with the right therapist.

  2. A joint session

    Your therapist meets you together and hears how each of you sees the relationship.

  3. One individual session each

    A chance to say what’s hard to say with your partner in the room.

  4. A plan, then the real work

    You get an honest picture of your patterns and a plan, then weekly sessions together.

Practical details

Couples therapy in Denver, Boulder and online

We see couples in person at our Denver office and at our Boulder office, and by secure video anywhere in Colorado. Online sessions work well for couples too. Some couples join from the same couch; others log in from two different places when schedules or travel make that easier.

Couples sessions are 50 minutes and run $180 to $200, depending on the therapist. We’re out of network with insurance and provide superbills, though most insurance plans don’t reimburse couples therapy, so it’s worth checking with yours. Appointments run from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. on weekdays, plus some weekend times, so an after-work session is usually easy to find.

Not sure your partner will come? Book the free 15-minute consultation on your own. We can talk through what’s going on, and if couples work isn’t possible right now, individual therapy can still change a lot about how you show up in the relationship.
