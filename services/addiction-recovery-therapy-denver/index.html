Recovery Without Shame

Addiction & Recovery Therapy

Substance use usually starts as a solution. It numbs something, quiets something, or gets you through something. Recovery means more than stopping. It means understanding what the substance was doing for you, and building a life that doesn’t need it to do that job anymore.

Who We Work With

You don’t need to hit bottom, have a diagnosis, or be sure you want to quit to start therapy. Wherever you are with it is a fine place to begin.

Questioning your useYou’re not sure it’s a problem, but you’ve noticed you drink or use more than you planned

Early recoveryThe first months, when everything feels raw and the old coping is gone

Long-term recoveryStaying steady years in, and dealing with what sobriety brought to the surface

Trauma and substance useWhen the substance has been managing pain from things that happened to you

Anxiety and depression in recoveryThe feelings that come back when the numbing stops

After a relapseGetting back on track without drowning in shame

✦   ✦   ✦

How We Work

Our work here is led by Kelli Ruhl, LPC, NCC, LAC, a Licensed Addiction Counselor in Colorado. Her foundation is person-centered: you’re the expert on your life, and you set the goals. She blends that with evidence-based skills and trauma-informed work.

Person-centered careNo lectures and no labels. We work on the goals you choose, at a pace that makes sense

DBT, ACT and CBT skillsPractical tools for cravings, urges, strong emotions, and the thoughts that pull you back

Parts workGetting curious about the part of you that reaches for the substance, and what it’s protecting

Somatic workCalming a nervous system that has relied on substances to settle

Recovery isn’t about becoming a different person. It’s about not needing to escape the one you are.

Addiction Therapy in Denver and Online

Kelli sees clients in person at our Denver office and via telehealth anywhere in Colorado. Therapy with us is outpatient counseling. If you need detox, medication support, or a higher level of care, we’ll help you think through options and can work alongside other providers.
