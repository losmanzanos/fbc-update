Where to begin

What one-on-one therapy is for

One person, one therapist, fifty minutes, and real room to work out what has been weighing on you.

People start therapy for all kinds of reasons. Some arrive in the middle of a crisis. Others come in because something quiet and persistent has been pulling at them for years. Most are somewhere in between, and you don’t need a diagnosis or a single clear problem to book a first session.

Individual therapy at Full Bloom is talk therapy with a plan behind it. Your therapist gets to know your history, your relationships and what you’d like to be different, then draws on approaches like EMDR, somatic work, Internal Family Systems, narrative therapy or CBT, depending on what fits. We think the relationship with your therapist matters as much as any method, so we take fit seriously from the first call.

We see clients in person in Denver and Boulder, and online anywhere in Colorado, seven days a week.

Some of what people bring in

These are the reasons we hear most often. Plenty of people arrive with two or three of them tangled together, and that’s fine.

  • Anxiety and chronic worry that keeps running in the background even when everything looks fine on paper
  • Depression and low mood, whether it arrived suddenly or built slowly over a few years
  • Trauma that still shapes how you think, feel and react, long after you thought you’d moved on
  • Relationship patterns with partners, family or coworkers that you keep finding yourself back inside
  • Life transitions like a move, a breakup, a loss or a new job, the moments that ask who you are now

  • Addiction recovery, for people who want support that goes further than staying sober
  • Identity and self-worth, working out what you think and want apart from what you were told to be

The first month

How it usually starts

  1. A free 15-minute call

    You tell us a little about what’s going on. We answer questions about fees, scheduling and approach, and suggest a therapist who fits.

  2. A first session that is mostly listening

    Your therapist asks what brought you in, about your history, and what you hope will change. You don’t need the right words or a tidy goal.

  3. A shared plan

    Within the first few sessions you’ll both have a sense of what you’re working on and which approaches make sense for it.

  4. Regular check-ins

    We ask what’s helping and what isn’t, and adjust as we go. You decide when you’ve gotten what you came for.

Before you book

Questions about individual therapy

Start with a free 15-minute consultation. We’ll ask what you’re looking for and match you with a therapist whose background, style and availability fit. You don’t have to figure this out alone. That’s part of our job.

It depends on what you’re working on. Some clients reach their goals in 12 to 16 sessions. Others choose to continue longer for ongoing support or deeper work. We check in on progress regularly and never keep you in therapy longer than you find it valuable.

Sessions are 50 minutes. Kelli, Kelsey, Kirsten, Jillian and Natalie charge $150, Mark charges $180, and Becca charges $200. We are out of network and don’t bill insurance directly, but we provide a superbill after each session that you can submit for reimbursement through your out-of-network benefits.
