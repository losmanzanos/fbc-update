Founder & Senior Clinician

Becca Moravec

LPC, LMFT, AAMFT Approved Supervisor

I’m a Denver therapist who works with millennial women, couples, and recovering people pleasers.

Most of the women I see are the reliable one. The friend who remembers every birthday. The coworker who quietly picks up the slack. The daughter still managing her parents’ feelings from two states away, and the partner who keeps the whole household calendar in her head. They’re very good at it. They’re also tired, and somewhere along the way they stopped being sure what they actually want.

As an (elder) millennial myself, I know this season of life from the inside: careers, kids or the question of kids, aging parents, and a group chat that never sleeps. I believe therapy isn’t about fixing you. It’s about helping you come home to yourself.

My Approach

My approach integrates Internal Family Systems (IFS), EMDR, the Enneagram, narrative therapy, and family systems. In plain English, we look at the parts of you running the show, the stories you were handed about who you’re supposed to be, and the family patterns you’d rather not repeat.

With couples, I’m interested in the fight underneath the fight. I’m trained in the Gottman Method (Levels I and II) and often bring in the Enneagram, which gives partners a shared language for why each of you reacts the way you do. As a Certified Intuitive Eating Counselor, I also help clients heal their relationship with food and body, because diet culture takes up a ridiculous amount of brain space.

You Might Be Here Because…

  • You’re the one everyone relies on, and you’re running on empty.
  • You say yes before you’ve checked in with yourself, and resent it later.
  • You know your Enneagram type and want to know what to actually do with it.
  • You and your partner keep having the same fight in different outfits.
  • You’re engaged and want to start your marriage with better tools than you were handed.
  • You’re tired of how much room food and your body take up in your head.
  • You became a parent and barely recognize your life, or yourself.

What It’s Like to Work With Me

Sessions with me are warm, honest, and conversational. I want you to feel like you’re talking with a real person, because you are. Some weeks we’ll trace a pattern all the way back to where it started. Other weeks we’ll get practical about the conversation you’re dreading this weekend.

I founded Full Bloom because I wanted a practice where therapy feels human and actually goes somewhere. I’m also the former co-host of the podcast Two Therapists in Therapy, where we talked openly about self-acceptance, growth, and relationships.

What to Expect Working with Becca

Becca offers therapy for women in Denver with a focus on identity, boundaries, and self-trust. She is particularly skilled with recovering people pleasers and high-achieving women who feel disconnected from themselves. Her trauma work draws on EMDR and Internal Family Systems, and she brings a deeply relational lens to couples therapy and pre-marital counseling. As a Certified Intuitive Eating Counselor, she also helps clients heal their relationship with food and body through a weight-neutral, HAES-informed approach.

Becca sees clients in person in Denver and via telehealth across Colorado. She is currently accepting new clients.
