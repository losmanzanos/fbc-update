Clinical Associate

Jillian Corpora

LPC

A lot of the people I see are new to therapy, or tried it once and it didn’t click. They’re anxious, or flat, or grieving, and they don’t know where to begin. That’s a perfectly good place to begin.

I work with teens, young adults, and adults navigating anxiety, depression, grief, ADHD, and big life transitions. I’m committed to helping people explore who they authentically want to be in the world. There are times when we all feel lost, stuck, and overwhelmed by everything we’re feeling, and therapy can help you stop fighting those feelings and start working with them.

Before I became a therapist, I worked with adults with disabilities and their families, helping people discover what they were capable of. Later I worked in hospice care as a grief counselor. Hospice taught me something I bring into every session: pain and loss deserve to be witnessed. It’s only then that there’s more room for healing, and eventually for joy.

I Specialize In

Anxiety & Depression

  • Worry that won’t switch off
  • Low mood, low energy, and losing interest in things you used to enjoy
  • Practical tools that fit your actual life

Grief & Loss

  • The death of someone close to you
  • The slow grief of watching someone you love get sick
  • Losses people don’t always recognize, like a friendship, a role, or a future you’d planned

Teens, Young Adults & Life Transitions

  • School stress, college, and first jobs
  • Moves, breakups, and the in-between stretches
  • Figuring out who you are and what you want next

ADHD & Neurodivergent Adults

  • The overwhelm of adulting with a brain that works differently
  • Missed deadlines, half-finished projects, and the shame that piles up around them
  • Building systems that fit how you actually think

Food & Body

  • Intuitive Eating and making peace with food
  • Disordered eating patterns
  • A kinder relationship with your body

My Approach

My style is warm, practical, and nonjudgmental. We’ll slow down, make sense of what you’re carrying, and build tools you can use between sessions. If you’re new to therapy, I’ll explain how it works as we go.

A lot of our work comes back to communication. Being able to say what you need honestly, to a partner, your family, friends, or coworkers, lets you live with more freedom and joy. I believe we all want meaningful connection, at home and at work, and that one skill makes life feel a lot less heavy.

You Might Be Here Because…

  • You’re not sure what’s wrong, only that you don’t feel like yourself.
  • You lost someone, and it feels like everyone else has moved on.
  • You’re in a transition, like graduating, moving, a breakup, or a new job, and feel unmoored.
  • Your mind keeps running worst-case scenarios.
  • You have ADHD, or suspect you do, and you’re tired of feeling behind.
  • You’re a parent looking for a therapist your teen will actually talk to.
  • Food and body worries take up more space than you’d like.

What It’s Like to Work With Me

Sessions with me are relaxed and down to earth. You don’t need the right words or a clear goal on day one. We’ll figure it out together, and I’ll check in along the way about what’s helping and what isn’t.

Clients newer to therapy, or unsure where to start, tend to find me easy to talk to. With teens and young adults, I keep things real and collaborative, so therapy feels like something they’re part of, not something being done to them.

A Little About Me

When I’m not working, you’ll often find me at Red Rocks, on a road trip somewhere in Colorado, reading on my back deck, or wandering the Denver Botanic Gardens.

Working with Jillian, What to Expect

Jillian provides warm, accessible therapy in Denver for clients navigating anxiety, depression, grief and loss, and major life transitions. She sees a lot of teens and young adults, works with ADHD, and is a Certified Intuitive Eating Counselor. Jillian is especially approachable for clients who are new to therapy or unsure where to start.

Jillian sees clients in person in Denver and via telehealth across Colorado. She is currently accepting new clients.
